      How these numbers are made
      • Scale: the person mask's head-to-heel span equals your height, giving centimetres per pixel at your body; every walk-around frame is re-scaled and leveled with the phone's gravity sensor.
      • Levels: pose landmarks place the neck, chest, waist (narrowest), hip (fullest), thigh, knee, calf and ankle.
      • Turntable girths: each frame gives a caliper width from one angle; a tape follows the convex outline, whose perimeter is the integral of those widths over half a turn (Cauchy's formula).
      • ± is a design estimate per method, not a validated accuracy.
